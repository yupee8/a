[Notes]
  • You can narrow down your search by selecting from "A" to "Wa".
  • You can narrow down your search by selecting a category.
  • Clicking on an item name will add it to the list.
  • If you cannot find the item you wish to dispose of, please cancel your application and contact the "Large Item Waste Collection Center" by phone.
  • Appliances covered by the Home Appliance Recycling Law (air conditioners, televisions, refrigerators/freezers, washing machines/clothes dryers) are considered bulky waste regardless of their size.
  • For appliances covered by the Home Appliance Recycling Law (air conditioners, televisions, refrigerators/freezers, washing machines/clothes dryers), a home appliance recycling ticket (recycling fee must be paid at a post office or Japan Post Bank counter) is also required.
  • You can order up to 5 items per product .
  • For items priced at 750 yen, you can order up to 4 items at a time.
  • For items priced at 1000 yen, 1250 yen, and 3000 yen, you can order up to 3 items at a time.
